About the programme

After decades building knowledge, skills and competence close to pregnant women, mothers, newborns, children and adolescents, two mentors decided to design and offer, as experienced consultants across countries, a mentoring programme having the Baby-Friendly Standards as pillars of maternal and neonatal health, aligned with the global Midwifery Models of Care.

The Maternal and Child Health Friendly Standards Mentoring Programme is an online programme designed to refresh, transform and strengthen the on-the-ground performance of frontline professionals in public or private health facilities with maternity wards — filling the gaps in essential, quality care for pregnant women, newborns and mothers.

Our vision, mission and goal

Vision

Love, passion and care as the gears to nurturing, saving and contributing to thriving mums and babies' lives across countries.

Mission

To improve maternal, neonatal and child health through mentoring frontline professionals.

Goal

Mums and babies are alive and thrive!
